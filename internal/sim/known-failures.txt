# Seeds that fail because of an open bug issue. scripts/sim.sh skips and
# reports them; ci fails on any other failure. One per line: the test, the
# seed and the issue, as in
#   TestSeeds 1234 https://github.com/djosh34/tigerstore/issues/357
# It holds seeds of internal/sim's tests that run sets of seeds
# (simrun.Seeds and simrun.Each); a test of one seed (simrun.Drive)
# cannot be listed. A package with such tests of its own, as
# internal/node, gets a file of its own.
# Under the race detector, where seeds do not replay, the runs leave out
# the faults of these bugs instead (raceLeavesOut).
